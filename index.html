How to play

Slide the numbered tiles around the grid. When two tiles with the same number touch they merge into one, doubling their value.

  1. Use the arrow keys or WASD, or swipe on a touch screen.
  2. Every tile slides as far as it can in that direction.
  3. Two equal tiles merge into one and add their value to your score.
  4. A new tile appears after each move that changes the board.
  5. Reach the 2048 tile to win, then keep going for a higher score.
  6. The game ends when the board fills and no move is left.
